the ladder

The LOD slider at the top of the panel is this table, live. Three of the four rungs are the same generator asked for less of itself -- a skirt is a surface of revolution with a frayed edge, so dropping spokes loses notches and keeps the cone, which is the whole argument for the scheme. What changes with the tier and is not geometry is the needle mat: LOD0 wears the cut-out one, and every tier past it wears the solid, because an alpha test at range is an aliasing machine. The last rung is one spun quad carrying a photograph of LOD0, baked here and now off the tree on the stage.

measured, in metres

Every shape slider is a fraction of height, so the proportions hold at any size -- which is what sizes is for. The crown numbers are read off the built mesh's own bounding box rather than predicted, because the fray moves the widest point and the lean moves where it sits.
