## Prompts that produced amazing songs

Nocturnal Pulse - chill modern watery poignant nostalgic electronic:

> melancholic indie electronic, nocturnal instrumental, E minor, 76 BPM half-time, hushed and introspective, uneasy, filtered shimmering synth pads, washed-out felt electric piano, warm sub bass drone, low-passed muffled kick like a heartbeat, distant reverb-soaked snare, ghostly wordless vocal texture, tape hiss, minor progression, tense and restrained, wide dark reverb, intimate

White Silence - spare, measured, chilly calmly adventurous snow biome world:

> Cold, spare exploration music for a vast snowfield -- hollow, still, and unobtrusive, no strong hook. Choose a fresh, unexpected key each time, not the default. A low bowed-string drone holds an open progression beneath a thin, distant high whistle and a few glassy bell tones that drift in and out. Entirely acoustic, wind-hollow and lonely, atmospheric rather than melodic. Around 70 BPM, evolving almost imperceptibly. It surges faintly once, then recedes -- restrained, calmly inspiring, and coldly mysterious throughout, never shouty or intense, the back half as calm as the opening. No fade-out; loopable, ending near the tonic. The rustle of chilly wind echoes in the background. No percussion.

The Last Forge - ominous ambience for an evil volcanic landscape:

> Bleak, melancholy exploration music for the ruins of a dead forge-land -- desolate, unobtrusive, and slow, no strong hook. Pick a fresh, unusual minor key, not the default. A distant, slow metallic clang echoes far off over a low ember-crackle and a deep drone; a lone mournful reed drifts a spare, grim line in and out. Entirely acoustic and earthy, hot, ruined, and sorrowful, atmospheric rather than melodic -- environmental, never a battle. Around 68 BPM, barely evolving. A dark swell rises faintly, then recedes -- restrained throughout, never shouty or intense, the back half as calm as the opening. No fade-out; loopable, ending near the tonic.

Glass Dunes (gorgeous adventurous sci-fi desert):

> Otherworldly, shimmering exploration music for a crystal-strewn desert -- mysterious, unobtrusive, and slow, no strong hook. Choose a fresh, unusual key each time, not the default. A faint heat-haze of glassy bells and bowed glass drifts over a low drone and a thread of wind, strange and weightless. A lone ney or duduk breathes a spare, modal line that comes and goes. Half-acoustic, half-glassy, wide and alien, atmospheric rather than melodic. Around 72 BPM, evolving slowly. It shimmers up faintly once, then recedes -- restrained throughout, never shouty or intense, the back half as calm as the opening. No fade-out; loopable, ending near the tonic.

Shimmering Wastes (gorgeous harsh desert):

> Arid, shimmering exploration music for a vast crystalline desert -- unobtrusive and slow, no strong hook. Choose a fresh, unusual key each time, not the default. A sparse hand drum ticks far apart over a low drone and a faint shimmer of heat-haze; a lone ney or duduk traces a spare, breathy, modal line that drifts in and out. Entirely acoustic and earthy -- wide, lonely, and atmospheric rather than melodic. Around 72 BPM, evolving slowly. It surges faintly once, then recedes -- restrained throughout, never shouty or intense, the back half as calm as the opening. No fade-out; loopable, ending near the tonic.

Abyss 1 (sinister gargantuan abyss):

> Raw, primordial acoustic music in a vast echoing cavern, ancient and eerie. A steady underbeat built from handpan and a deep frame drum, ringing into reverberant stone. An open repeating drone-based progression in D minor, sparse and unhurried. A bowed erhu plays a lonely, rough-edged melody, breathy and human, echoing alone in the enormous dark. A didgeridoo holds a low drone beneath. Around 84 BPM, entirely acoustic and earthy — small and human against something immense and old. A brief swell of strings rises only near the end, then recedes.

Caverns (adventurous, exciting, marveling):

> Delicate, raw acoustic music, uncanny and luminous, echoing in a vast cave. A quiet handpan underbeat woven with soft struck stone and clay percussion. An open modal progression in A minor touched with major, spare and strange. A kalimba and a hammered dulcimer play a fragile, simple, ringing melody, alone in the enormous echoing dark. Around 84 BPM, earthy and acoustic — intimate and bare, beautiful in an alien, primordial way. A faint string swell appears only at the close.
