the ladder, for this tree

Every row is read live: the rungs and their ranges are LOD_BANDS and DRAW_RADIUS in src/v2/render/trees.js, and the triangles are this tree at this height rather than a class average. The two mesh tiers are the SAME tree: LOD1 only drops the trunk to three sides and each limb to one upright fin, so every leaf card stays in its seat and the swap at the first boundary moves nothing you can see. Everything past them is one triangle carrying a side-on photograph of LOD0, spun to face you, one bake and one texture layer per species — and where the species stands its triangle apex-down, the card is sunk below the ground so the trunk still has width where it meets it. The camera stays where you left it when you flip tiers, so you can watch for the moment one stops being convincing.

measured, in metres

Every shape slider is a fraction of height, so proportions hold at any size, while branches and sprays are counts stated at heightRef and scale with height: a 3 m tree is not a shrunk 25 m one, it has fewer of everything at the same real density. These are the numbers that say whether a size is believable -- a 25 m pine with a 15 cm trunk is not. Use sizes to see the ladder side by side.

crown profile

Branch length against height. This is the macro shape: crownPeak moves where the widest branch sits (0 = cone, 0.5 = round, 1 = inverted), crownFullness sets how fast it falls away from there.

textures

Leaf colour, leaf alpha, bark. Cut from EZ-Tree's art by tools/trees/gen-layers.mjs and loaded through the same loadImageLayers path the game uses. The middle pane is the one that matters: a leaf card is a rectangle, and the whole silhouette of a canopy lives in that channel.
