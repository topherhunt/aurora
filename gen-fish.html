Each generate click makes one real OpenRouter call (FLUX.2 Klein 4B, ~$0.015 at 1MP) -- nothing fires on its own. A fish only needs one view (side) -- there's no second view to stay consistent with, unlike the character sheets' front/side/back. Picking a candidate saves it immediately to tools/fauna/sheets/<id>/side.png (gitignored -- a working source, not a shipped asset).

The picked side view is stored at its natural, generated color -- alpha-keyed but not flattened to grayscale. The tint preview panel shows that same cutout with a live CSS hue-rotate on top, so different individuals of the same species can be given random hue variation without needing separate art per color variant. At strength 0 the preview matches the stored art exactly; strength only controls how saturated the re-hue reads.

The mesh preview panel lofts a low-poly 3D body straight from the picked side view's contour (tools/fauna/loft-fish-mesh.mjs): dorsal-ventral height comes right off the silhouette, lateral thickness is a heuristic bulge since there's no top-down view to measure it from. Every vertex carries a swim-bend weight that grows toward the tail, so "swim wiggle" previews the fin-propulsion motion the mesh is built to support at runtime -- no skeleton needed, just a per-vertex sideways offset driven by that weight.
