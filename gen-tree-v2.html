what is different

Same species, same seed, same wood. Only the leaf cards change. v1 hangs a rectangle of the boxed cut, a fixed number per limb, folded about the diagonal its two triangles already share. v2 hangs the warped cut on the four corners it was projected from, so drawing it undoes the projection and the art comes back at its true proportions with roughly twice the opaque area; the stem corner sits exactly on the limb; cards are spaced every sprayEvery metres of limb rather than counted; and the crease runs stem to tip, folded a little, usually downward.

built, per tree

Read off geometry.userData.tree, not off resolveTree: v2's card count is a function of limb length and cannot be predicted without building, so the triangle law still prices it as v1. Triangles per unit of leaf area is the number that decides this -- if v2 needs fewer cards to read as a crown, it wins twice, once on triangles and once on the overdraw that is actually costing the frame.

the two cuts

Top row colour, bottom row alpha. Left the boxed cut v1 draws, right the warped cut v2 draws, with its opaque fraction underneath. The warp is a homography: the four hand-marked corners of the spray go to the four corners of the square, so the texels the boxed cut spent on air become leaf.
