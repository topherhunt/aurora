what this is

v2a is v2's warped-cut sprays, hung every sprayEvery metres along each limb, plus one card per limb drawn before them: stem corner on the trunk, tip at the branch tip, so its stem-to-tip crease is the branch. It lies level, rolled off it by limbTilt, and folds downward 20 to 40 degrees every time -- this card is the branch, and a branch does not open upward. The small sprays then hang along that same crease, thinning and drooping the higher up the tree their limb starts, which is what keeps the top pointy.

built

Read off geometry.userData.tree, not off resolveTree: the card count cannot be predicted without building. There is exactly one limb card per limb, so the limb count is that row, and every other card is a small spray. If the limb card carries the branch, the small ones can be thinned until it does most of the work.

the cut

Colour over alpha, at the cutoff the cards are drawn at. Every card on the tree is this one cut; the limb card is simply one of them drawn very large. That is the bet: a spray card's cost is its screen area, so one big card at 60% opaque buys more canopy than four small ones do.
