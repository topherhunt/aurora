edits

Saving writes rig-edit.json next to the rig and applies it to rig-fixed.glb. glTF deforms a vertex by world matrix times inverse bind matrix, and every edit here keeps that product the same at rest: reparenting rewrites the local transform, deleting hands the joint's weights up to the joint that adopts its children, and moving recomputes the inverse bind matrix so only the pivot changes.
