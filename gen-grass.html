this frame

Frame ms is the main thread, top of the animation callback to the bottom of render(). Desktop numbers, so read them as a ratio between settings rather than as a Quest prediction -- the point of this page is the geometry, and the headset is where the frame gets judged.

the bed

density by ring

The law is keep = min(1, F / d): full density inside full radius, then halving every time the distance doubles. That is already what src/v2/render/grass.js does, so the ring counts here are directly comparable to the card bed's. blades/m² is the number that decides whether a ring reads as lush.

against the card bed

The card bed's figures are measured, from scripts/check-grass.mjs and the fill probe: 10,993 cards, 22.0k triangles, and 20.6 full eyes of alpha-tested fragments against a tuft texture that is 18.9% opaque. Fill is what a Quest 2 runs out of; the geometry bed spends triangles instead, and the triangle line is the one to watch.

what is not being tested here

Aliasing. A blade goes under a pixel at about 5 m on a Quest 2, and an opaque sub-pixel triangle has no mip chain, so the far field will crawl in the headset in a way a desktop monitor at this resolution will not show you. That, and not fill, is the thing that may still want a ring of distant cards. Also not here: the tiled camera-following scatter and the regrow queue -- this page re-scatters the whole disc when you have walked far enough, and reports the cost when it does.
