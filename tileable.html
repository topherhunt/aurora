Roll moves what was on the border into the middle. A tile is seamless when the rolled view shows no cross.

Fractions of the image, so they hold at any size. Inset is how much of the original centre is kept untouched; blur is how softly it fades into the rolled copy. Keep blur under a third of inset -- past that the fade reaches the border, the original's non-wrapping edge leaks back in and the tile stops being seamless.
