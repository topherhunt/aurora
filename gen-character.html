this bench

Every character shares one bone hierarchy (tools/characters/rig.mjs), placed at proportional fractions of the measured figure. That's usually close but not exact -- this bench nudges one character's bone positions without touching its mesh. save writes public/characters/<id>/rig-override.json (bone name -> local translation), which generate-character.mjs applies on top of the proportional placement the next time that character is regenerated.

Skin weights are computed from the proportional placement, not the override -- moving a bone here changes where it sits at rest but not which vertices follow it when a clip animates. Good for a bind-pose correction (a shoulder that reads too narrow); a large move will show its seams once the character walks.
