# §30 -- Leafkin and the leafkin villages

Every large forest has a hidden leafkin village: grubby elf-men a metre tall who grunt, squeak and pant to themselves while they bumble through the wood collecting mushrooms. See one and it squeals, drops what it carries and runs for home. Home is a dark hole low in the side of a giant boulder, easy to miss, and walking into that hole puts her inside the boulder: a glade of fern-lined winding paths, tall trees, a stream running to a small lake, and a clearing of shabby stump-huts around one great one. The leafkin are not friendly unless she brings them something they want. Later: leafkin at home in the village, and trade. Built in three pieces, each a thing on its own: the entrances, the leafkin, the village -- the last being the first **room** that is not the overworld.

## 1. The entrances

### Siting: the `hollow` rock bed

The entrance boulder needs a near-vertical face at ground level, deep in a wood, and must look like every other big rock there. §25's giants cannot promise a flat flank at a known height, so the entrances are a bed of their own: **one per 300 m tile that holds a wood**, the tile's one candidate put by `deep` at the point of the tile furthest from open ground and the tile refused unless that point is 50 m into the forest (the forest law's, which runs 55 m up into peak ground), `maxSlopeDeg: 20`, not submerged, `lie: true` (the shape's middle axis up: the longest level like a lying giant's, and the bank boulder's flat bed face -- measured 2 x 1.44 x 1.16 -- standing as a wall), the burial pinned at `sinkRange [0.45, 0.46]` (just under half, never squashed, so the face is the hull's own), `anchor` and `blocks`, resident to 1250 m. A pure function of the tile seed like every bed. `scripts/probe-villages.mjs` counts the shipped map's (287 boulders, 278 of them with a face the mouth probe accepts; the blind ones sit on 14-27° ground) and lists them nearest a point; the `critter LOD tint` row paints them purple (`Rocks.setHollowTint`).

### The mouth

`Entrances` owns no scatter: every frame it asks the rocks for the resident hollows within `RADIUS_M` (400) and seats a mouth on each new one, so a site is where its boulder is and goes when the boulder goes. The face is found by rays against the boulder's own hull from a bearing rolled off the site's key (`PROBE`: 1 m out, eye 0.75 m, 1.5 m of wall within 20° of vertical, up to 16 bearings -- the flat flank is a narrow target on a lying boulder, and the smallest, 8 m, shows 3.2 m of stone), so two clients agree on the mouth to the bit. The mouth is the shipped `cave-mouth` arch scaled to `MOUTH_HEIGHT_M` 1.5, sunk 0.1 m into the face along the normal, drawn on the props' ladder with the shipped T3 in the card's place (an arch is on a wall; a card spun to her would stand out of it), and a **black polygon** -- unlit, `fog: false`, `HOLE.outline` in the arch's frame -- a hair proud of the stone across the arch's passage rather than a cavity: the depth test keeps it in front of the face and the arch's ring around it, and the parallax across the arch's protruding half metre makes it read as a hole. The arch is a solid mound with a recess, not a through hole, and its ring thins at the sides and runs out above ~1 m, so the polygon is cut **inside the ring's solid** at one plane, 0.14 m into the arch's frame (`MOUTH_SINK_M` + `HOLE.proud` 0.04): 0.1-1.1 m over the arch's base, 1.22 m across, the left shoulder notched under a seam that opens 0.1 past the plane. Wherever it reached past the ring it was a black shape on the boulder, and wherever it fell short a rim of stone showed inside the ring, so `check-entrances` samples the pick itself (parity of ray crossings through the shipped T0) and demands stone at every vertex and every centimetre of edge across the planes 0.12-0.16, and that the outline grown 1.5 cm still fits. The face is probed at the outline's vertices and its box centre, since a bulging face is proudest at the centre; how far the stone stands proud of the face point's plane (`bulge`, at most `HOLE.maxProud` 0.2, and at most `maxBulge` 0.4 deeper than the deepest probe) brings the arch, the hole and the mouth point forward together, so the hole keeps its one plane in the ring on every face. **Flanking stones** (`FLANK`): 1-2 a side, rolled off the key, 4.8-8.8 m across, the boulder's own placed colour (`Rocks.hollowTintAt`, its roll rather than `tintAt`'s middle), the rocks' own boulder on the rocks' own material (`Rocks.boulder()`, the tiers cloned because PropMeshes hangs its fade attribute on what it is given), each on the rung its own size and distance earn under `ROCK_LOD_AT` like any rock (the arch's rungs are scaled to a 1.5 m arch and put a 6 m stone on its 20-face tier at 10 m), each set along the face from the ring outward where a ray along the normal meets the boulder, leaning `lean` 0.4 of its radius into it and bedded `sink` 0.4 of its height, and each a column to the walker (`walk.addStone(entrances)`, culled by the site's `flankReach`), so the mouth is tucked away and found only by one who knows what to look for.

`sites(into)` yields `{ key, x, y, z, nx, nz, r, ax, ay, az, holeX, holeZ, state, flank, flankReach }`: the **mouth point**, `MOUTH_STEP_M` 0.7 plus the bulge in from the face along the outward normal, for the leafkin; the arch's centre; and the **hole point** on the face, for the portal. A room passes `fixed: [site]` instead and gets that one mouth, at that point, on nothing.

### The portal test

The slope limiter is not replaced: `player.js` refuses the step into the face and slides, as it must at every other boulder. `portalTest` runs after the step: for a site within `PORTAL.reach` 20 m (`PORTAL` lives in `entrances.js`), she enters when her feet are within `walk` 0.5 m of the hole point and the step ran into the face (`-(step · n) / |step| >= into` 0.5), or a teleport landed within `blink` 0.8 m; the door she stands in takes her once a visit (`portalIn`). The limiter stops her feet 0.05-0.54 m from the hole on the shipped map, so `walk` is as near as she can get; `check-entrances` and `check-village` walk a `Player` at the face and demand it. Into a village she arrives at its own exit; out of one she arrives at the mouth she came in by (`cameInBy`). Refused in the editor.

## 2. The leafkin

### Pipeline

`rig-map.mjs` derives `rig-map.json` from the Tripo biped rig (the `--forward` flag names the facing axis where the rig's own guess is wrong), the skin ladder cuts the tiers, the clip solver bakes the human library plus two new specs, and `ship-biped.mjs` with `'leafkin'` in `BIPEDS` writes `public/creatures/leafkin.glb`. The two clips are human-library specs, so every biped gets them: **`run-carry`** (the run gait with the arms held still, raised and bent so the hands meet at the chest; its speed is the run's) and **`gather`** (a pose clip, `loops: false`, ~0.9 s: crouch, spine forward, one arm to the ground; the key at t 0.5 is the reach). **The human run is a run**: `run.json` at duration 0.5, stride 1.0, duty 0.32 (6.25 wheelbases a second, 1.84x the old 0.7/0.95/0.4, which was slower than her walk), and the leafkin's `clipTweaks` run and run-carry at stride 0.7, duration 0.32, duty 0.36 -- short quick steps, a skittering little thing at 3.25 m/s against her 1.45 m/s walk. Every biped ships the same clips, so all eleven were re-shipped with the library, and `avatars.json` with them.

### Runtime: `leafkin.js`

Snowmen's puppet, tiers, FootIK and fade, with the scatter replaced by **one leafkin per resident entrance site** (`MAX` 16: the 400 m entrance radius on the 300 m tiling), seeded from the site key and its spawn tick, spawned at a random point of its `ROAM_M` 150 disc at least `SPAWN_CLEAR_M` 15 from her feet, so it is never seen to appear, and only where a walk out from the mouth ends (`_search`): a target drawn in the disc past `SPAWN_MIN_M` 40, the walk started on the passable cell nearest the mouth inside `FINAL_M` and stepping over the flee's own `CELL` grid with its corner rule, each step the unvisited passable neighbour nearest the target's bearing, `SPAWN_STEP` 100 steps a frame, so wherever it stops the flee's planner is known to reach the mouth from it -- 250-950 `seat` probes and 1-5 frames a spawn over a wood with a lake in it. A walk ending inside `SPAWN_MIN_M` fails, and `SPAWN_FAILS` 5 failures (or no ground at all within `FINAL_M` of the mouth) leave the village empty `EMPTY_S` with one `console.warn`, shipped to the dev server's `tmp/client-log.txt` by log-ship.js, so a site that cannot seat a leafkin is on record). It steps on the score's fixed ticks (`sim/score.js`) so two instances stepped on different frame times agree to the bit; `SIZE_M` 1 ±15%; drawn only within `critterTier`'s cull (~36 m for a metre of body), minded as long as its site is resident. It never walks: every gait is the run.

| state | what | leaves when |
|---|---|---|
| **roam** | a target inside the site's `ROAM_M` 150 disc every `RETARGET_S` 5-15 s, run (run-carry with a bundle) on a heading that random-walks about the bearing (`WOBBLE_*`), so the path arcs and doubles; every step probed on the walk surface, a refused probe turning it away by `DETOUR`, `REFUSALS` 10 in a row picking a new target | a cap within `SEEK_M` 3 -> gather; her feet within `STARTLE_M` 3 of its feet -> startle |
| **gather** | run to the cap, the gather clip, `mushrooms.take` at `GATHER_KEY`, the cap into the bundle (`CARRY_MAX`); then the next cap in reach, else roam. A full bundle stops the gathering. Mushrooms are only drawn within ~55 m of her, so only those are gathered | no cap in reach -> roam; she is near -> startle |
| **startle** | stop, face her, recoil, the bundle scattered, a scream; `STARTLE_S` 1 | -> flee |
| **flee** | run home along a path planned over the walker's ground (`planPath`: A* on a `CELL` 0.5 m grid about the site, a cell passable where `seat` stands, no corner cut, at most `PLAN_OPEN` 800 cells opened before it settles for the one nearest home and plans again from there), weaving about it by the roam's wobble scaled to `FLEE_WOBBLE` 25°, running straight at the furthest of the next `LOOKAHEAD` 8 waypoints with a clear line, `REPLAN_TICKS` 10 refused steps planning again from where it stands -- a slide along whatever was in the way circled for good in a pocket between two boulders and a trunk; within `FINAL_M` 1.2 of the mouth point, straight at the arch with no step probe (the walker's ground there is the boulder's top, and the probe refused the very step home), its feet capped at the arch's floor | within `HOME_M` 0.3 of the arch, or out past its cull -> gone |
| **gone** | despawned; the site empty `EMPTY_S` 300 | the time up and her inside `ROAM_M` -> the search for a spot with a way home, and a fresh spawn there |

A leafkin is not constructed in a room (`ROOMS.leafkin.leafkin` false); the village has its villagers instead.

### The villagers: `villagers.js`

The leafkin at home: one a house and `EXTRA` 1 more (the spare shares house `k % houses`), the same puppet, ladder, materials and voice as the gatherer, stepped under the `leafkin` visibility row and on the score's fixed ticks. **They walk the roads and nothing else.** The build's roads are dry, clear of trunks and houses and hold a footpath's grade by construction, so a villager never probes the ground: `roadGraph` flattens every road but the pads at `SAMPLE_M` 1.5 into chained nodes, joins each road's ends to the nearest node of another road (or its own ≥ 3 nodes off) within `JOIN_M` 3, and hangs every house door on its nearest node, and every trip is a `dijkstra` path over that graph, followed at `NODE_M` 0.35 with the walk gait (`_follow`, the heading turned at `TURN_RATE`). The trunk is never a wander target, so nobody walks out to the exit. Four **gazing spots** are spread round the loop, each `GAZE_OFF_M` 1 m off the road toward the lake where that is dry (the one step off the cobbles a villager takes).

| state | what | leaves when |
|---|---|---|
| **inside** | hidden (not drawn, not heard, no puppet) at its door; `INSIDE_S` 20-90 s | -> an errand |
| **walk** | a trip along the graph to an errand's end, walk clip; every `MEET_TICKS` looking for another villager walking or standing within `TALK_M` 1.6 with both `talked` cooldowns run down | arrived -> what the errand was for; a meeting -> talk |
| **stand** | idle, `STAND_S` 3-10 s | -> an errand |
| **gaze** | at a spot, turned to the lake, idle, `GAZE_S` 15-40 s | -> back onto the road and an errand |
| **talk** | a pair stopped facing each other for a shared `TALK_S` 8-20 s, one of the four `talk-*` gestures with each chatter (`leafkinChatter1-4`, `CHATTER_S` 1.5-3 s apart, the two half a gap out of step), idle between; `TALK_COOL_S` 45 before either talks again | the time up -> both stand |
| **flee** | her feet within `STARTLE_M` 3 (feet to feet, in three dimensions): any talk broken off and the run gait along the graph with nodes within `SHUN_M` 6 of her feet weighted `SHUN` 10x, a whimper and a pant by turns; home and hide if its door is `CALM_M` 30 from her, else to the farthest node within `FLEE_M` 60 m of road; a villager never stands its ground (a node with nowhere farther to run is a graph bug and throws) | arrived home -> inside; arrived elsewhere with her `CALM_M` off -> an errand, nearer -> run on |

An **errand** (`ERRANDS`) is home 0.2 (the trip ends inside), a gaze 0.3 or a wander 0.5 to a random node off the trunk (ends standing). **Every client sees the same day**: each villager reseeds its PRNG at every `CHAPTER_S` 600 s chapter from `hash32(seed, id, chapter)`, a boot mid-chapter replays the chapter from its start in one frame with an unbounded budget (`stepTo`, `Infinity`) and its voices are dropped when more than `SILENT_TICKS` were stepped, so a village entered at any moment is already about its day and silent about the replay. The startles are the one thing local: her feet are hers, and a flee on one client is a walk on another until the next chapter turn puts them back together. `bodies` and `voices` feed the ambience like the gatherer's (`FOOTFALLS.human` on the walk and the run, every call a `SOUNDS` name); `PUPPETS` 8 puppets are shared among however many are out, the rest stepped but not drawn (`stats.starved`).

Gate: `check-villagers.mjs` builds one real village and runs the villagers on the gatherer's stand-in asset: the graph covers every road but the pads and every door in one piece with every node on a centreline and dry; a 600 s day with her far off in which every villager goes in and out of its own door, nobody steps off the cobbles but at a spot, walkers walk and standers idle, a gazer faces the lake, every talk is a facing pair within reach who both chatter and gesture, every call is a sound the ear has and none comes from indoors; two instances on different frame times agree to the bit and a boot 500 s into a chapter finds them out and silent; and her feet beside a walker set every one running, whimpering and panting by turns and nothing else heard of it, none calming under 30 m and all past it, each getting away from her or indoors.

### Carrying and dropping: `hands.js`

`carry(owner, span)` returns a carrier -- `add(record, src)`, `place(x, y, z, yaw, chest)` per frame, `count()`, `scatter()` -- drawn by the hands' item pool. Everything it takes is resized to `span` (the record itself, so the thing stays that size scattered and in her hand; the leafkin's is `CARRY_SPAN` 0.14 of its body, half its torso's width) and held against the chest at `CARRY_SLOTS`, each jittered and given its own roll about the body's forward axis and tilt about its side, so an armful is a jumble and not one shape drawn five times; `scatter()` moves them into the loose list in state `fall` with random headings, so they fall, thud and roll with the drop physics and are pickable after. Carriers are taken lazily and held to `CARRIERS` 16, one per resident leafkin, since a leafkin gathers whether or not it is drawn; `POOL_CAP` grows by `CARRY_MAX * CARRIERS`.

### Voice: `ambience.js`

A herd entry `{ layer: leafkin, clips: 'human' }` for the feet (`run-carry` on the run's beats); the one-shots (a call and a pant by turns, `CHATTER_S` 2-3.5 s apart roaming and `WHIMPER_S` 2.7-4.1 s apart in flight, the chatter or the whimper first; a squeal at a cap in sight, no oftener than `SQUEAL_S` 5 s; the scream) drained from the layer's `voices(into)` with a position, so the sound engine and the state machine never share a clock. The pant is a one-shot at the voice's level, not a loop: a loop at breathing level was never heard over the chatter. The six `.m4a` were re-encoded to `.mp3`; `npc-leafkin-panting.mp3` was a genuine MP3 and plays as is.

### Multiplayer

The seed makes every client spawn the same leafkin at the same site, and nothing keeps their states together: your leafkin flees, mine goes on gathering; a dropped bundle is local too. The leafkin adopts creature sync (`_notes/creature-sync.md`, `src/sim/score.js`) when it lands rather than growing a third pattern.

## 3. The village: the first room

### What a room is

A room is what `ROOMS[id]` names: the overworld (`dir: 'world'`, its files, sky, hollows, leafkin) and the village (`village: true`, built in memory at boot). `bootWorld` reads the save and builds the room it names; `bootRoom(room, site)` is the swap: the view faded to black over `FADE_MS` 500 (a sphere on the camera whose opacity the tick steps, so it holds in XR; a hidden tab, which ticks no frame, snaps it after two fades' time), every layer disposed, the room's stack rebuilt (`buildRoom`), her feet `ARRIVE_M` 2 out from the site along its normal, facing along it (`faceAlong`), and the view faded back in over another 500 ms once she can move. The clock, the backpack and the net come with her; what her hands hold is packed and restored (`restoreHeld`), except a creature, whose layer is gone -- it goes in the backpack if there is room, else it is lost. The banks (atlas, GLBs, clips) stay loaded, so the swap is the terrain build and the placement. The save carries `room` and `door`, the mouth she came in by, since that is the village to build; a village save without a door boots the overworld with a warning. In a village the animals are the small ones: the wildlife holds fox and hare only (`Wildlife`'s `species` option), no roosts and so no dragons, and the fish, frogs, crabs and insects as the overworld has them. Positions still cross the net without a room tag: a peer in the village is drawn to an overworld player near the origin, and vice versa; a `room` id on the pose is the deferred first cut.

### The valley: `rooms/village.js`

**Every village is its own.** The seed is `keyHash` of the mouth's site key, the entrance's place in the overworld, so a village is the same on every visit and every client and no two are alike. `rollVillage(seed, house)` rolls the shell's yaw, the jitter's seed, how many rivers (`RIVER.count` 1-2), their bearings and each one's swing, wavelength and phase, how far the loop stands off the water (`LOOP.over` 2-4 m), which side the clearing is and how far round (`CLEARING.bearing` 50-120° off the exit's), the ring houses' count and heights and which is the great house, and the outlying houses' count and heights (`OUTLYING.count` 2-3). `buildVillage({ spec, shell, house })` answers `{ heightmap, doc, spawn, exit, clearing, lake, props, lamps, ground, spec, fishSeed }`, `spec` being the roll actually built: a roll whose lake is a puddle, whose houses do not fit round the ring clear of the wall and `EXIT.houses` 20 m from the exit, whose rivers none reaches the lake or winds too little, whose rivers leave no room for the loop, whose trunk finds no way down clear of them and the clearing, whose loop or trunk steepens past `ROAD_GRADE` between its held chords or holds one bearing for `STRAIGHT_M`, whose trunk crosses the loop, whose roads cannot hold their grade or whose outlying house finds no site is rolled again from the next `attempt` under the same shell (`ROLL_TRIES` 128; the six gate seeds take 0-12 re-rolls, 60-380 ms cold, and the count is chaotic: any constant moved re-rolls everything after it). `fishSeed` is the first seed from the room's up whose first bass site on one of the four fish tiles about the lake stands in water deep enough with clearance (`fishSeedFor`, fish.js's own roll replayed), since a pond a few hundred square metres wide holds such a site for one roll in ten and a room seeded blind was as often fishless as not; main.js seeds the room's `Fish` on it, so every village has a school. Nothing in the height stack knows it is a room: the heightmap is a 1025² `Heightmap` over `WORLD_SIZE` at the overworld's 8 m texel, the layers a doc in the overworld's format, and the field the same `V2Height` with the shipped relief, so the 10 cm detail, the road carving and the lake and river cuts are the overworld's. The terrain worker seeds itself with `SEED` and calibrates the detail from the heightmap it is sent, so the map itself must calibrate like the overworld's (a jittered cone measures about half the island's roughness; `CALIBRATION_LEAST` 0.4). **The tile repeats**: the hollow is one `TILE_TEXELS` 16 (128 m) tile evaluated once about the origin and laid across the map, since the field's altitude bands and calibration read the whole map and a map that is one value with a hole in it hands them nothing; the climb past the wall ends in a flat plateau so the tile is periodic, kept under the 65 % of the map `V2Height.bands` lets sit at one height, and nothing outside the shell is ever seen. The whole 4 km map is a constraint of the engine, not a choice: `WORLD_SIZE` is asserted across the height stack, and the 4 MB Float32 heightmap is the price of reusing it unchanged.

The ground, in metres about the room's axis, is `makeGround(shell, spec)`:

- **The cone**: its tip `FLOOR` 60 m on the axis; its rim `DROP` 10 m over that on the mean, scaled on each bearing by the wall's radius there over its mean (the wall read once at `FLOOR + DROP`, kept across a seed's re-rolls), so the ground climbs at one grade on every side and stands highest where the boulder is longest (`RIM.stretch` 1.25 the most a radius may stand over the mean), plus `RIM.amp` 2 m of value noise round the circle (6 and 12 rolled values, weighted by halves), so the ground meets the stone at a different height on every side and no straight boulder-to-ground line shows; the tip rounded to a hyperbola over `TIP` 0.15 of the rim's radius so the lake bed is not a point. 10 m over a 29-40 m radius is 14-19° on the cone, under the forest law's 32° over most of the hollow (the seeds grow 58-80 trees, down to the shore and up to the huts' backs) and 7-12 % of the dry hollow over her 50° walk slope (`STEEP_SHARE` 0.2 in the gate; the roads' banks, the river banks and the sub-texel detail add to the cone). A 20 m drop (the first cut) was a wall of 27-35° with the wood pushed onto the rim's shelf and a quarter of the ground unwalkable. **The jitter is the only smoothing**: one lattice per octave at `JITTER.spacings` 32, 16 and 8 m, periodic on the tile, every vertex moved by up to `amp` a tenth of its octave's spacing either way, bilinear between vertices, the finest lattice the texel grid itself so the map holds it exactly; it dies over the last `foot` 12 m to the wall.
- **The rim and the climb**: the wall is read on every degree at that degree's rim height and the rim set `PAST.in` 3.5 m inside it, and from the rim the ground climbs at `grade` 1.4 (54°, over her 50°) for `fade` 12 m into the stone, flat beyond at `TOP`. The 8 m texels smear the kink at the rim by up to 3 m and the wall leans in above the rim by up to 0.6 m a metre: read at the wall itself, the smeared ground stood out through the stone, unroofed, and at 3 m in one seed's last walkable metre at the rim stood under 2.9 m of stone. The gate wants the ground in the stone within `ENTER_M` 3 m past where the wall was read and never out of it again, and `HEADROOM_M` 3 m of roof over every walkable metre.
- **Water**: the lake is the water standing `LAKE.over` 3 m over the lowest ground `margin` 12 m inside the wall, drawn as one uncarved plane across the whole hollow (water shows wherever the ground is under it), so its shore is wherever the jitter put it; the largest pool under that level on a 1 m grid is the lake (`findLake`), under `least` 120 m² a puddle (the seeds' lakes are 170-270 m²). A lake 4 m over the floor was tried for the fish and not taken: fewer trees, fewer frog seats, more re-rolls, and still only half the seeds seeded a school. **The rivers wind.** Each comes down from a source `RIVER.from` 0.85 of the way to the wall on a bearing within `sector` 40° of straight away from the exit, no two nearer than `apart` 30°, walked on the bare field (the lake alone) half a metre at a time: every step downhill, on the fall line pulled toward the lake's deepest point by `follow` 1.5 and swung across it by a sine of the river's rolled `swing` 50-75° over its `wavelength` 10-16 m, the swing shallowed until the step descends (it rarely is: over 3400 steps gated, 250 shallowed once and 40 more); stalled `stall` 12 steps in a pit it runs straight for the deepest point; in the water it runs on until the ground stands `into` 0.8 m under the lake, and that is the mouth, so the sheet dives under the lake's plane and no joint shows where river meets lake (paths.js reads a mouth on the lake wherever the ground is within a channel depth of its level, and never raises one). A node every `node` 4 steps (2 m, a fifth of the shortest wave) keeps the bends in the spline: a node every 4 m flattened them, and a rolled drift off the fall line (tried) added nothing the node density had not. A bearing whose walk reaches no water of the lake's own, or runs under `wind` 1.15 times its chord (a phase that cancels over so short a run comes down all but straight), is stepped `retry` 5° either side; the seeds' rivers run 1.18-1.48 times their chords. **No road comes within `clear` 6 m of a river**: half a road, its `ROAD_FEATHER` 4 m, half a river's bank and a metre; the feather, laid over the carve, would lift the bed out of the water.
- **Paths**: 1 m roads with a 4 m feather (footpaths in a bowl 60 m across, not the overworld's 8 m shoulders), all **wandering** (`WANDER` λ 12 m, ~1.2 m, sampled an eighth of a wave apart, the push grown in over the first half wave and died over the last so a road ending on another meets it on its plan's bearing) so no `STRAIGHT_M` 8 m run stays within `STRAIGHT_DEG` 6° of one bearing, each profiled over `PROFILE_M` 24 m of the ground along it and its chords held to 20° by cut and fill (`roadThrough`; the spline steepens a few degrees over a chord, `ROAD_GRADE` 25°), pinned exactly where it meets another. **The loop** (`d2`) rounds the lake `LOOP.over` past the water on every bearing (the wet radius dilated and averaged over `smooth` 30°), from where it stands `RIVER.clear` off the rivers on one side of their span to where it does on the other, more than a half turn, through the exit's bearing and the clearing's. It stands at two levels: **the terrace**, the ring's height, for `LOOP.level` 15° either side of the clearing's bearing and wherever it passes within the houses' reach of the clearing (paths.js `smoothRoads` reads the nearest road alone, and a loop falling past a house's wall tilted its footprint by 0.8 m), and everywhere else **the shore**, cut level `LOOP.dry` 1 m over the water, its feather the gentle shelf down to the waterline the frogs sit on; between the two the loop is left free for a ramp's length and falls at the chord grade. **The trunk** (`d1`) comes down from the arrival to the loop on the exit's bearing in legs sweeping up to `TRUNK.sweep` 110° round the lake and back, as many as hold the drop at 20° with `slack` 1.1 and no more than 4, hairpins on 2.5 m, the last `approach` 8 m straight in so it meets the loop square, on the side away from the clearing or the other where that runs through the rivers' wood, cut as one constant-grade ramp from the exit's `arrivalY` (the bare ground `ARRIVE_M` in, or whatever higher stands the face within a chord's rise of it) to the loop's. **The spur** (`d4`) leaves the loop on the clearing's bearing, `CLEARING.spur` 2 m straight out to the ring's inner point; **the ring** (`d3`) is the clearing's 10 m circle, and the spur, the ring and the loop's terrace stretch are all at one height, the ground's mean over that terrace, `CLEARING.dry` 1 m over the lake at the least, so the loop is built up over the water by about what the clearing is cut into the slope behind it (4-6 m of cut, 2.5-4 m of fill on the seeds; a terrace at the loop's own level cut 6-10 m behind the houses). **The branches** (`d5`, `d6`, ...) leave the loop for each outlying house, one grade from the loop's height to the door's with the first `OUTLYING.apron` 4 m held at the loop's, wobbling half the wander. **No road crosses another** (`roadsCross`, `JUNCTION_M` 1): the ground takes the nearest road's height, so two roads crossing at two heights would be a broken bridge, and roads meet only where one ends on the other. The trunk's approach and the fade of the wander keep the trunk off the loop, and a trunk that crosses it anyway is rolled again; a branch is planned from the loop point nearest the rolled site within `window` 10 points either way from which one grade reaches the door crossing nothing, so a branch over the loop is refused before it is a road. Nothing else is a road: the houses' sides and backs are grass and wood.
- **The clearing and the houses**: one clearing, a disc of `CLEARING.r` 5 m the wood keeps off, on the rolled bearing or the nearest within `CLEARING.bearing` stepped `sweep` 5° at a time, either side, where the houses stand `wall` 1.5 m clear of the stone; ringed by the path with `HUTS.count` 5-6 houses round it, doors on the ring facing its centre, the great house (`GREAT_HUT` 6-6.5 m) on a flank (at the head, across from the lake, it would be the one house nearest the wall) and the rest packed out from it to either side in turn (`housesRound`), `gap` 1 m between neighbours and as much from the spur and the loop. A house is half as wide as it is tall, so six round a ring this size with the spur's wedge fit only small: `HUTS.height` is 4-6 m for five and 4-4.5 m for six. **The outlying houses** (`OUTLYING`, 2-3 of them, 4-6 m) stand off the loop on rolled bearings, `off` 3-6 m out from it to the door and along it by that times the tangent of `skew` 50-65°, so the branch ramps slantwise across a slope too steep to climb straight (a shallower skew leaves the loop so nearly along it that the two roads' wander crossed them); each tries the shore first half the time (`shore` 0.5), down in a bay between the loop and the water where the loop's radius leaves one dry, else the wood; `gap` 2 m clear of every road and house, `wall` 2 m clear of the stone, `EXIT.houses` 20 m from the exit like every house (so she comes down through the wood before the village shows; the gate wants trees on the line between them), `tries` 120 sites before the village goes without it. Every house stands level on a **pad**: two ring roads hidden under its floor at its road's height (the ring's for the ring houses, the ground under its centre for an outlying one), `pads` 0.25 and 0.7 of its radius out and `padHalf` a quarter of it wide, so the smooth (the nearest road alone) levels the footprint out to the walls and the cobble (`SWELL`, a fifth) never shows past them.
- **Scatter**: the same rocks (hollows off: no village inside a village), trees, ferns, grass, mushrooms, litter and deadwood against the room's field, the wood kept off the clearing alone (`villageBiome`, `RoomProps.occupiesAt`) and the huts through the trees' occupier; the rest of the hollow, to the houses' backs, is wood. **The creatures** are the overworld's layers on the room's field and water: grasshoppers (10-30 about the lake), crabs on the placed rocks' perches (10-20), frogs on the shore's shelf (`frogs.js` seats on dry ground under 35° within 5 m of the water, off the stone: the seeds hold 100-180 m² of seat and roll 0-6 frogs each, 17 over six, since a frog is a candidate rolled anywhere on its 8 m tile at `DENSITY` 0.025 and kept only on a seat), and fish on the build's `fishSeed`, so every seed seeds a school. **The pond is clear**: main.js sets the water's clarity to `VILLAGE_POND` (0.7, seen into from 15° below the horizontal, where the overworld's lakes are mirrors until she dives) and draws the fish from above the surface in a room, since a pond 20 m across is looked into from its shore at 20-30°. Its ambience is the quiet lapping alone (`Ambience` `waves: false`): the wave one-shot never breaks on a shore this small. A pond-size threshold for that, rather than a room flag, is on the wishlist.

### The shell, the light, the exit

The **shell** (`shell.js`) is **the rock bank's boulder she walked into**, on the bank's own stone layer, stood as the hollow bed stands it (a Z quarter turn), at `SHELL.scale` 55 (80 m on its long axis, the wall 29-40 m from the axis at the rim) with `sink` 0.4 of its height under the floor, turned inside out (index winding reversed and normals negated: a negative scale alone does not expose the inside, three flips the front face for it), front-face-only, the stone the boulder's own tile at the stood scale (a placed boulder's tile scales with the rock), and tinted as a placed boulder is (`Rocks.tintAt`, the bank shared: `rocks.bank === shell's`). `wallAt(y, bearing)` and `roofAt(x, y, z)` are rays against it, and the gate demands 3 m of roof over every walkable point. **The shell is stone to her** (`walk.addStone(shell)`): a column table over the hull's plan at `CELL`, each column the heights the vertical line through it crosses the surface, so below the first crossing, above the last and every second gap between is stone (`columnAt`, `blockTopAt`, `crossingsAt`; a wall bulging into the room is a ledge on that line), read bilinearly where the four cells about a point agree on their count and from the nearest cell where they do not. The wall refuses her step like any boulder, and her flight is held under the roof: `WalkSurface.ceilingAt` is the underside of the lowest stone over a point, `Player._fly` keeps her head the same clearance under it that her feet keep over the ground, and a column with no room for both (the foot of the wall) refuses the step unless she was already in one, so a step out of stone is never refused the way a step in is. Within `EXIT.door` 1.5 m of the exit's face point the stone gives way top to bottom (`setDoor`) for the arch and its hole.

The **light** is the overworld's: the same clock, hour, sun, hemisphere, fog and palette, unchanged, since the clock comes with her through the door. `sinkCave` only hides the sky, the stars and the aurora, which the shell closes over; the weather holds cover and rain at zero in a room and the wreaths off. A lit hole in the roof is a later pass.

### The lamps: `render/lamps.js`

Hide-hooded flames on `LAMP.height` 2 m posts, placed by the build (`placeLamps`, `LAMPS`): one beside every house door, `door` 1.2 m along the wall from it with its foot at the ring's edge, and one every `spacing` 10 m along every road but the pads, `verge` 1 m off the centreline on alternate sides (the other side where that fails), none within `apart` 4 m of another (a house's within `doorApart` 3: neighbours a `HUTS.gap` apart put their door lamps nearer than the verge's), on a road, in a house, in the water or within `wall` 1.5 m of the stone; a place that fails is skipped, not moved (the gate allows a `LAMPS_GAP_M` 20 m dark stretch and sees 0-3). The posts are one static InstancedMesh (the placeholder mesh in `buildPost`, to be replaced by a real one), the flames another, unlit and unfogged so a flame is the brightest thing in the glade from across it; a post is stone to the walker and a trunk keeps off it. **Lit only after dark**: the glow runs from `lit` dayness 0.6 down to 0.15, and by day the flames and their light are out. **Their light is not point lights**: every lit material shares one program, and twenty distances per vertex of every tree and per fragment of the ground is not a Quest 2 budget. It is a map baked once over the room's plan at `texel` 0.5 m, the sum of each lamp's falloff to `reach` 9 m at `gain` 1.2, three channels carrying three flicker groups so `uLampGlow` can flicker them out of step (`FLICKER`, two sines about a mean, real time), the alpha the flames' height so a roof ten metres over a lamp stays dark and the hut wall beside it does not; `lighting.js` reads it as a third compile-time axis (`setLamps`/`clearLamps`, the vertex path reading it per vertex and carrying the light across), so the overworld pays nothing for it, and the light multiplies the surface's own colour, which is what makes it read as a flame's and not as the night lift. `check-shaders` compiles the lamp axis; `check-village` gates the placement and the map (bright at every foot, lit within the reach and dark past it).

The **exit** is the same arch and quad, one fixed site from the build's `exit` (`placeExit`): the bearing whose wall stands most nearly plumb across the arch's height (`EXIT.band` 1.5 m, within `plumb` 0.5 m; every `sweep` 4° round, then every degree about the best, the band read at its ends and middle and the rest only where that spread still beats the best, since thirteen rays a bearing is most of a build), its face point on the wall at the arch's mid height over the ground there as the trunk's shoulder will leave it (the bare ground blended toward the arrival's, `ARRIVE_M` 2 m in, by the road feather), so the arch's back half stands in the stone and the mouth is never loose on the ground; its normal into the room; the same portal test sends her home.

### The score: `audio/music.js` (unwired, no songs)

A glade is meant to have music and the overworld none, but **no song auditioned so far is the glade's**, so `SONGS` is empty, nothing in `main.js` constructs a `Music`, `public/music/` is gone and the gate is off `npm run check`. The module keeps the player for when the right songs arrive. **The playlist is a timetable and she keeps to it**: a `timeline` over the room's world clock (`clock.seconds`, the same on every client in a netplay room) seeded by the village's seed, slot k a `PAUSE_S` 1-5 min silence rolled from `hash32(seed, k)` and then a song rolled from it that is never the one before, each slot starting where the last ends on the pinned lengths in `SONGS` (afinfo's, not the decoder's, so every browser cuts the same slots), so the second every song starts on is fixed by the seed and every client hears it start together. A song is only heard from its beginning: entering mid-song or mid-pause plays nothing until the next slot's second (a start reached within `LATE_S` 2 s still plays from the top; one the frame loop slept through is passed over for the one after), and a song that has started plays to the element's `ended` unless she leaves or the switch (`setEnabled`, for a menu row beside Sound) goes off, either of which ramps the level `LEVEL` 0.125 to 0 over `FADE_OUT_S` 3 and pauses the element when the ramp lands; entering again mid-fade cuts it dead, and back on she waits for the next start like anyone walking in. The songs stream through `HTMLMediaElement`s (a decoded two-minute song is 45 MB of PCM) into one gain on the master bus: the Sound row's mute takes them, the water does not, and the next song is told to fetch during its pause. A song that fails to load turns the music off until the next room and says so once. Wiring it back is one `Music` on the `SoundEngine` at boot, `enter(seed)` in `buildRoom`, `leave()` in `disposeRoom` and `update(clock.seconds)` in the frame loop.

Gate: `check-music.mjs` (by hand) runs the timeline on its numbers and the player against a fake engine and fake elements on a fixture pool, frame by frame on a world clock, for every rule above, and checks whatever `SONGS` lists exists under `public/`.

## Decisions taken

- The room is built in memory in the overworld's format at the overworld's 8 m texel; nothing ships for it.
- The bowl is a valley of its own inside the bank's boulder, not the boulder's underside; the shell is that boulder's mesh and stone.
- The light inside is the overworld's, on the same clock; only the sky, stars and aurora are hidden. The lamps add a baked map, never point lights.
- The shell is stone to her: no walking or flying through the wall or the roof.
- The villagers keep to the roads and are local under a startle; the day itself is shared through the seeded chapters.
- A village pond hears the quiet lapping only; a size threshold for the wave is wishlisted, not built.
- `hollow` is a rock bed.
- The `house-leafkin` mesh ships; no stand-in huts.
- The mouth is 1.5 m and she does not duck.
- The startle range is measured feet to feet: the leafkin's ground point against her plan position, never her head.
- The leafkin is per client until creature sync lands (the aurora-game-22 build); the seed keeps the same leafkin at the same site on every client.
- `npc-leafkin-panting.mp3` is a valid MP3 and is used as is.
