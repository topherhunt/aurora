# Castles

Procedural castles: a 3D path graph of rooms and edges (`src/v2/castles/layout.js`), the exterior and interior shells it implies (`shell.js`), previewed in `gen-castle.html`. Gated by `scripts/check-castle-layout.mjs`.

## Layout

A ring of corner towers joined by curtain walls round a court, plus keep, gatehouse, mid towers, hall and one lookout whose summit holds the chest. The lookout is always part of the castle, either a raised back corner or a tower against the keep's back, and never stands on its own. Cells are 4 m, storeys 4 m; walls are 2.4 m thick and two storeys high. Rooms are one section-storey each, about 28-45 a castle (typically ~34). The interior is the layout scaled by 2 across and 1.5 up (6 m storeys), so it is roomier than the outside but keeps every direction and adjacency. A stair is a 2 m spiral of 15 segments about its cell's centre, each 1/16 turn of 2 treads (0.2 m rise), so stairs stacked in one cell make one helix; the slab above is open only over its high steps, and the rest of the disc is the landing. A post no spiral climbs on from ends in a finial. A roof ladder stands in a cell corner, or on an axis in a round room, clear of any spiral's hole. The maze score (route length, in/out switches) passes about 19 seeds in 20.

## Shell

Sections are lofted footprints: the rect with each corner cut askew in two facets, or a 12/16-gon for round towers (half the one-cell towers), sides broken at half cells. Rings: a footing 6 m below ground, a root crown spreading 0.8 m at grade and gone by 2.4 m, a narrow jittered waist, a slow swell, then a corbel band flaring 0.7 m (hall 0.25, wall outer face 0.45; a wall's court face spreads at most 0.3). Door leaves stand 0.3 m proud of their wall (`doorDepth`, past the crown at the ground): wall faces are flat between warped vertices metres apart, while each leaf corner warps on its own, so a thinner margin lets the wall sag over the leaves. Used roofs get a parapet ring of merlons, each flared and jittered so none is square. A wall's parapet is lofted on the wall's own stations and top-ring vertices, so it bends with the warp and leaves no gap underneath; unused tower roofs get a two-stage concave spire. Bridges are decks on a stone arch springing from the faces they join, its rise capped to keep a door's height clear over the walk or roof below (`clear`). The court is a separate flagstone mesh (the road's cobble tile, 3 m): a solid slab under the whole court rect, plus a 30 deg ramp out of every door the entry node uses, which runs 11 m so it dives into a falling hillside. The ramp is cambered with bevelled edges, jittered stations and heights, and widens by 0.9 m toward its foot. Masonry uses `public/buildings/castle_stone.webp` (FLUX, made seamless, 256 px, 4 m a tile; `tools/buildings/castle-textures.mjs`). One position-only warp (`src/buildings/v2/warp.js`) bends everything, keeping coincident vertices coincident.

Z-fighting rules, enforced by the coplanar-overlap gate on the unwarped shell:
- A box side a tall neighbour stands against is a hard plane for every ring, and only one parapet stands on it (the taller section's, on a tie the earlier). The roof edge along it splits where the neighbour across it changes and cedes only where that neighbour wins: a stretch past the neighbour's end keeps its parapet, else it opens on the drop. A round neighbour meets the plane only near its middle, so a box cedes to it there (ROUND_MEET either side, room for its door) and nowhere else, and never takes a box's parapet.
- Anything butting masonry (door frames, bridge ends) runs into it rather than ending on its plane.
- Landings stop 8 cm short of the cap they meet: the warp bends two tessellations of one plane apart by a few cm, so even same-coloured coplanar faces would flicker.
- Two flights never share court (layout claims each flight's plan rect).

Crack rule, enforced by the seam gate (no T-junction between faces turned alike opens 6 cm under the warp): the warp moves each vertex on its own, so a vertex partway along another face's edge opens a sky-coloured crack. Faces that continue one another share every vertex on their seam: a section's parapet cuts run down every ring and through its roof, clamped rings collapse a fold rather than double back, a spire skirt on a clamped side starts from the ring's own vertices, a bridge's sides, rails and deck share the arch's stations, and each step's side carries the corner of the step behind. Separate closed bodies (a lintel on its jambs) may meet unshared. What remains (under 4 cm) is roof meeting roof between equal sections, seen only from above.

## Siting

`src/v2/layers/castles.js` `planCastles`, run in the world-plan boot: at most one castle per 1400 m region (75% of regions), its seed the first of up to 8 rolls that passes the maze score. Candidates every 40 m are ranked by prominence (at least 3 m over a ring 120 m out), clear of labyrinths, towns (radius + 60 m) and their keep-outs; the first with a yaw at which `fitAt` holds wins. `fitAt` asks that the ground under the whole footprint lies within the footing (5.5 m) below the floor, which stands `CASTLE.raise` (1.2 m) over the sited ground, and that the gate ramp's foot meets the ground. Castles shape no ground. Each gets a trail from its gate ramp's foot to the road network, as labyrinth gates do. `CastleSites.occupiesAt` is the footprint keep-out: trees, rocks and deadwood read it through `BiomeField.castleAt`, wildlife through main.js `wildAvoid`.

## Rooms and props

`furnish.js` `roomPlan` cuts each room node into chambers with stone partitions (`PARTITION`: 0.4 m thick, a 1.6 m doorway at one cell's middle), cut on cell lines: the throne's node into pieces of at most 6 cells, the mess's 4, the rest 2 or 1 (round rooms and the gate's ground room stay whole). Roles are assigned once each: throne (biggest keep chamber on storey 1), kitchen and pantry, the bedchambers, then extras (chapel, maproom, treasury next to the throne, infirmary, library, bakehouse, cellar, workshop); every leftover chamber cycles through generic ground, upper and tower-top roles. Props are records `{ kind, x, y, z, yaw, w, d }` in the court, on the roofs and walks, and in every chamber by its role's kit (`must` pieces, wall kinds, a centre piece, a second pass of wall kinds, tableware on tables and dressers). Stores run to grain sacks, crates of one crop each, kegs, jar shelves, coal bins and hanging hams; living rooms to chairs and dressers. A chamber holds at most 2 torches (sconces, braziers). The court's training ground takes a corner far from the gate, its archery targets leant on the masonry. Every portal keeps a clear disc and every pair of a chamber's portals a clear lane, so no prop blocks the maze. `shell.js` meshes them in its own palette: court props are baked into the exterior's full tier, room props into the interior. The town interior builders are not reused.

## Light

Every interior and dungeon vertex carries a baked `light` attribute [fill, torch, window], which the shader (`render/castles.js` `interiorMaterials`) turns into `uFill*x + uFire*y*flicker + uSky*z` plus her held torch (`render/interior.js` `HELD_TORCH_GLSL`), the only dynamic light. `castles/light.js` `interiorLight` bakes the rooms: fill dimmed by ambient occlusion toward corners, each flame falling off as 1/(1+(d/1.0)^2) with wrap so the wall a sconce hangs on catches it, and each window's spill. `castleWindows` cuts one per cell side that looks out where nothing tall stands; a pane is a vertex with z past GLASS, drawn as the hour's sky light (`skyThroughGlass`, as town houses). Faces are cut into 2 m cells (4 m on ceilings) to carry the light, on the bilinear surface of their warped corners. A prop takes one sample for all its vertices: from its floor, or the wall behind it for what hangs high, or the ceiling for a chandelier. The dungeon bakes fill and torch the same way in `dungeon-mesh.js`, its prop vertices also from the floor. Interiors run to ~58k triangles (worst ~78k).

## Dungeon

`dungeon.js` `dungeonPlan`: a 1 m grid under the whole castle, floors about y -6 (storey k -1). A spiral (continuing one already rising from the room, if there is one) leads down from a corner tower's ground room to an 8 m foot chamber; a locked door on its side toward the castle's middle opens into a maze. The maze is a lattice of nodes 8 m apart (12% dropped, none near the foot) joined by a depth-first spanning tree plus loops. Each node is a 3 x 3 junction (40%) or a room of 2-6 cells a side, 2.8-4.6 m high; passages are 2-3 wide and 2.5-3.6 m high. The tree's last 6 edges toward its deepest node are the drain: 3 wide with a gutter of water down its middle. 20% of rooms are flooded and some passages puddled. The chest stands in a dry room at least 4 x 4, against its rock.

`dungeon-mesh.js` draws floors, water, walls (with steps up and down to neighbouring floors and ceilings) and ceilings, the spiral and the props (`furnish.js` `dungeonProps`: sconces by the door and in about a third of rooms, stores, wall chains, straw, bones, stove-in barrels, cages, the chest). Light (see Light) is baked before the warp: ambient 0.24 plus each sconce flame and a light down the stairwell, falling off as 1/(1+(d/2.6)^2) and shadowed by marching the grid; vertices near water are tinted with moss. Water is a separate mesh drawn with the overworld's cubemap water (ripple, Fresnel, mirror cross-fade): each castle with water gets its own `WorldProbe` while she is inside, captured from above the water nearest her within 30 m, and the overworld probe pauses. The warp then bends walls with `interiorWarp` and jitters their upper parts by up to 0.15 m, except within 1.5 m of the foot and the door, whose stone meets other meshes. Props move rigidly with their origin's warp. About 13k triangles (max ~17k). The door is a separate mesh (double leaves, iron lock plates), removed once unlocked.

Collision: `interiorSolids` adds the spiral as a pseudo stair edge, the maze's air whenever storey -1 is among those opened, prop boxes, and a 0.2 m lock box across the doorway that `unlocked` disables. `portals().dungeon` gives the door, the key's spot and the chest top. The key (`keySpot`) lies on a chest or table in the throne room 70% of the time (else in the treasury or bedchamber first), on the floor when nothing there has a top. The ghost names where it is. Touching the door with the key in a hand or the backpack unlocks it; without it, a caption says it won't budge. Loot ledger bits at place `k` plus the region: 0-3 the summit chest, 8 the key, 9 and 10 the dungeon chest's 60-120 coins and HOARD gem, and 16 the door unlocked, which `openCastle` re-applies.

## Runtime

`src/v2/render/castles.js`, bands in `CASTLE_BANDS`:
- **Far card:** seed 3's exterior and court, photographed once from the gate side (ortho, alpha-cut, dilated). Each site gets 2 crossed instances at yaw and yaw+90 deg, scaled by its size, until its meshes draw. Seen obliquely, a crossed card shows its edge.
- **Build:** within 220 m (dropped past 260) `castles/worker.js` builds the layout, both exterior tiers, the court, the lit interior, the dungeon and its water, and the solids off the main thread, about 170 ms a castle. A castle that throws there replies with its error; the site keeps its card and the error is thrown on the main thread with its seed. In the worker bundle three is a stub (`vite.config.js` `noThreeInWorkers`), so shell.js emits plain arrays and `toGeometry` makes the BufferGeometry on the main thread.
- **Meshes:** drawn within 200 m. The full exterior within 50 m, the mid tier (no merlons, slits, bars or court props) past 55. The mid tier saves only 20-30% of triangles (full 6.8-11k), and draw calls dominate at that range.
- **Interior:** one mesh in the interior material (see Light) plus the dungeon's water, set down `CASTLE_RISE` (320 m) over the site while she is inside. Then main.js `stepInCastle` hides the overworld each frame, keeping only her, the castle's drawn meshes and what moves in it, and the weather is stilled.

## Garrison

`render/castle-folk.js`, local to each client and set by the clock: at least 10 folk a castle besides two travellers on its trail, each in a townsfolk body. Inside: the lord and his steward in the throne hall, a cook, the kitchen maid, servants at chores and a guard. Outside: 3 or more guards pacing the wall walks, two at the gate, one at the well. The maid (`maidSays`) tells of noises behind the dungeon door, the ghost if there is one, and the room the key lies in. Some pace back and forth on a path by the clock; the rest stand at their posts. There is no town sim behind them, but each speaks once as she comes within `FOLK.near`, on their body's voice pack: a castle line (`castle.*` moments in `tools/voice/atoms.js`, `FOLK_SAYS`) 70% of the time, else an everyday one, and a gate guard halts her first. The ghost whispers a `castle.ghost` line with each line it speaks.

## Collision and portals

`solids.js` reduces each castle to plain data. The exterior is section bodies, crowns and battlement bands, plus boxes (flight treads, bridge decks and rails, hatches, the court slab, the chest, court props), ramps, and door notches cut through them. The interior is room and doorway air, everything else solid, plus each spiral's steps, post and hole air, and prop boxes; only the storeys within one of her feet are opened, which keeps a column under the walk's 8-span cap. `Castles` is a stone layer on the overworld walk. Inside, a `CastleInteriorStone` drives a walk of its own, swapped in and out as a house's is (main.js `openCastle` / `closeCastle`).

Spirals and court flights are walked: their tops are decks (`deckAt`), so the walk takes each step as level floor (a 1.4 m flight's slope samples would otherwise meet the court either side). The teleport lob falls 0.26 m a sample, past a 0.2 m tread, so `CastleInteriorStone.stairAt` (solids.js `spiralTreadAt`) sets it on the tread it crosses. Every non-barred outside door is closed by its leaves, solid on both sides: a door's is one quad of `public/buildings/door.png`; the gate's is one arched plane of `public/buildings/castle_gate.png` (FLUX, cropped to the doors, 128 px; `tools/buildings/castle-textures.mjs gen-gate`), its arch rising `GATE_ARCH` of the height to match the picture, with stone spandrels up to the lintel. `portals()` gives each door its leaves' exterior and interior faces with a standable arrival each, a blink for every ladder (inside at its foot, outside at its roof hatch), the rope and the chest. The rope fires when she walks at it; doors fire on touching their leaves (within 0.5 m of the face), the rest on entering their zone. Arrivals land outside their zone, and `arrived()` marks the zone she lands in as current, so a portal never sends her straight back.

The summit chest holds 40-90 coins and 2-3 HOARD gems, at loot place `k` keyed on the region (main.js `layLoot`). The rope lies coiled on the summit roof until she clicks it, then hangs for the session (`ropesDown`) and lets her down the lookout's face through `Climb.startRope`.
