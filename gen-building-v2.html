the warp field

One displacement field over space, applied to the finished vertex array, normals recomputed after. Because it is keyed on POSITION, two vertices that were coincident get the same answer and the shell stays airtight -- which is why the crookedness can be this large without opening a seam. Set strength to 0 to see the straight building underneath it.

what the plan decided

LOD by re-generation

Not decimation. The collapse decimator refuses to collapse an edge that borders a hole, and a building is holes by construction. So the far tiers are the same plan built with less of it -- detail 1 drops the bevelling, the rounding and the 3D joinery, and every window, frame and shutter becomes one flat rectangle. Every tier is warped by the same field, so the silhouette does not pop on the swap.

the tiles it wears
